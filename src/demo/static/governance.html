TAKE IT INTO THE ENTERPRISE

            
A governance blueprint, beyond this demo.

            

              Assign an owner to each boundary and carry identity, purpose and
              evidence across them.
            

          

          

            Approved AI inventory & access
            

              Register models, agents, MCP servers and tool owners. Use managed
              identities, approved provider routes and controlled network
              egress. The lab's shared sign-in and signing key are teaching
              conveniences, not enterprise identity management.
            

          

          

            Data protection & responsible use
            

              Classify data before inference; minimize fields, redact sensitive
              content, set provider retention and residency rules, and review
              high-impact actions. This case demo minimizes its input but does
              not implement a DLP scanner or prove prompt-injection prevention.
            

          

          

            Policy, audit & operations
            

              Version and test tool policies; bind approval to exact arguments;
              deny when policy is unavailable. Correlate gateway traces with
              durable business records. Define audit access, retention, incident
              response and evaluations for each use case; traces alone do not
              establish compliance.
